Artifact Title

Machine Learning vs. Deep Learning Industry Analysis: Retail & E-Commerce

Introduction

This collaborative presentation analyzes how machine learning and deep learning are transforming retail and e-commerce by comparing traditional demand forecasting with large-scale visual search.

Description

The artifact examines the retail data landscape, operational challenges, real-world AI applications, model fit, interpretability, computing cost, training requirements, ethical considerations, and an adoption roadmap.

Objective

The objective was to determine when retailers should use traditional machine learning and when deep learning is the stronger choice based on data type, complexity, explainability, cost, scale, and business value.

Process

Our team researched retail applications, selected Walmart demand forecasting and Alibaba Pailitao visual search as contrasting cases, reviewed peer-reviewed evidence, compared algorithms and trade-offs, evaluated whether the approaches could be exchanged, developed a staged adoption strategy, and verified the citations and AI-use disclosure. I served as the comparativist and strategist.

Tools and Technologies Used

Microsoft PowerPoint, Consensus, Semantic Scholar, Google Scholar, Claude-assisted drafting and layout, tree ensembles, gradient boosting, random forests, convolutional neural networks, comparative analysis, and APA 7th-edition citation review.

Target Audience

Retail and e-commerce leaders, data and ML practitioners, technology strategists, instructors, and students who must choose an appropriate AI approach for a real operational problem.

Challenge

Retailers must choose between traditional machine learning and deep learning for problems involving fundamentally different data, costs, interpretability requirements, and operational risks.

Solution

This presentation compares Walmart demand forecasting with Alibaba visual search and recommends a staged approach: use efficient, explainable machine learning for structured data and adopt deep learning when complex unstructured data justifies the additional investment.

Value Proposition

The artifact translates technical model-selection differences into an evidence-based business strategy that decision-makers can understand and apply.

Unique Value

Its unique value is a side-by-side comparison of two real companies and two fundamentally different data problems, including an honest discussion of cost, interpretability, ethics, and circumstances in which the methods may overlap.

Relevance

This artifact demonstrates comparative AI/ML analysis, structured-versus-unstructured data reasoning, academic research, strategic technology adoption, collaborative presentation, and responsible decision-making.
